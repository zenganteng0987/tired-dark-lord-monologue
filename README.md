# The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue

Entry for the make.fun competition "The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue" (slug: `u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb`).

## Entry

> Come in. Mind the— yes, the skulls. I keep meaning to move them. Sit. No, not there. That chair eats people. The other one. Good. You want the crown, the curse, the whole dark lord performance, I know. I rehearsed it once, in the mirror, when the mirror still flattered me. These days I sign decrees with a quill that leaks, I eat standing up, I dream in paperwork. The ravens bring me treason before breakfast. Treason! Before breakfast. So. Kneel, or don't. The dungeon's full, the torturer's on holiday, and hero— if you kill me, you inherit the filing.

A 100-word monologue spoken by a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired. The tiredness lives in the rhythm, the pauses and the small deflections — never in a plain statement that he is weary.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Requires Node 22.18 or later. It prints the monologue exactly to stdout and nothing else.
